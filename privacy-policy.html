Vibe Tutor Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-27

Overview

Vibe Tutor (com.vibetech.tutor) is an educational app from Vibe Tech LLC for teens ages 13–17. It helps with organization and study, gaming-themed rewards, optional AI chats, and optional internet radio. It is not directed to children under 13. The app does not create accounts. You may optionally enter a display name, which is stored on your device for personalization. The app does not ask for email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, or call and SMS logs, or serve ads. We do not sell personal data.

Who we are

Developer: Vibe Tech LLC, 2529 William Brunson Rd, Summerton, SC, USA. App: Vibe Tutor (com.vibetech.tutor). Privacy contact: freshwaterbruce2@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Most data stays in app storage or a local SQLite database: an optional display name, homework and assignments, schedules and study progress, rewards and achievements, preferences and Parent Zone PIN settings, Tutor and Buddy chat history, learning statistics, and a random installation identifier. Learning statistics are not sent to us. Data Management offers Export Data and Import Data for files you choose to create; do not treat them as a guaranteed backup or restore service.

Data that leaves your device

The app uses HTTPS and blocks unencrypted HTTP. App verification sends a random installation identifier and a Google Play Integrity token to our backend. The server retains only a one-way keyed hash of the identifier and issues a short-lived session token. When you use AI, messages and recent context plus the assignment, homework, progress, or schedule details needed for that feature leave the device. Optional reports send a category and include message text only when you choose to attach it. The backend performs automated content-safety checks and may send hard-to-classify messages for separate automated safety review. Optional radio and web font requests go directly to their hosts.

AI processing

The app sends AI requests to our backend only and never contacts AI providers directly. Our backend forwards requests to OpenRouter, currently using DeepSeek first and Google Gemini as fallback. We request zero data retention and no training use; providers process requests under their own terms. Usage limits apply to AI features while core non-AI features remain available. Content-free operational metrics may include outcome, model tier, tokens, cost, and timing, but no chat text or installation identifier. We do not log chat text.

Microphone and voice input

Voice input for homework is optional. Device system speech recognition, which may be provided by Google, may process audio under its own terms. Vibe Tutor receives the resulting transcript text and never uploads or stores microphone audio. Typing remains available. Vibe Tutor does not request camera access.

Internet radio

When you play a station, your device connects directly to SomaFM, LISTEN.moe, or WQXR. That host receives your IP address and basic request information. Vibe Tutor does not relay audio through our servers or send listening activity to us.

Service providers

OpenRouter and its AI model providers process optional AI requests. Google provides Play Integrity, Cloud Run hosting, and Firestore storage of counters and reports. Radio stream hosts and web font hosts (Google Fonts and cdnfonts.com) receive your IP address and standard request information when contacted. These providers may process data in other countries under their own terms and policies.

What our servers store, and for how long

Daily and monthly AI usage counters in Firestore are linked only to the hashed installation identifier and may remain after their periods. Reports include category, hashed identifier, and submission time; attached text is marked to expire 30 days after submission, but automatic removal depends on cloud retention settings not yet confirmed. Session tokens expire automatically. Hosting request logs may include IP address, path, time, and user agent under Google Cloud retention settings. We do not keep account profiles.

Deleting your data

Uninstall Vibe Tutor or use Android Settings > Apps > Vibe Tutor > Storage > Clear storage to remove all local data, including chat history, schedules, learning statistics, and installation identifier. In the app, Parent Dashboard > Data Management > Reset App clears homework, achievements, points, rewards, and sensory settings. It does not clear chat history, schedules, learning statistics, or the installation identifier. In Data Management, ‘Delete my server data’ requests deletion of server reports, pending AI request records, and usage counters that the service can identify for this installation. Older usage counters, platform logs, backups, and provider records may remain. This does not erase data stored on your device or immediately revoke an existing session. If deletion cannot be confirmed, the app asks you to try again later.

Your choices

You may skip AI, voice input, and radio; core study features continue without them. You may deny microphone permission, reset specified app data, clear storage, or uninstall.

Security

The app uses HTTPS, short-lived backend session tokens, a PIN-protected Parent Zone, usage limits, and automated content-safety checks. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Age scope

Vibe Tutor is intended for teens ages 13–17 and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 provided personal information, contact freshwaterbruce2@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change and update its effective date.
